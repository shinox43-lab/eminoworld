私隱政策

We are committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how Emino World Limited and its affiliated entities (“EMINO WORLD”, “we”, or “us”) collect, use, share and protect your personal information, including information we collect through our website, the products and services we offer, and your interactions with us (collectively, the “Services”).

01 — Collection of personal information

We collect personal information directly from you, and automatically through your use of and access to our Services. You provide most of this information directly — for example your name, company or brand, email address, telephone number and project details — when you submit an enquiry form, brief us on a project, subscribe to updates, or otherwise communicate with us. We also collect information indirectly through your interactions with the Website, which may include your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring pages and time stamps. We use this information to respond to you, establish and maintain our working relationship, and improve and customise your experience.

02 — Cookies

We and our third-party service providers use cookies and similar technologies to operate the Website, remember your preferences and language, understand how the Website is used, and improve your experience. Cookies are small text files sent from web servers that may be stored on your device; they do not contain account or password information. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings, although some features of the Website may not function properly if you do.

03 — How we use and share information

We use personal information to respond to your enquiries; to deliver, operate, enhance and improve our Services; to communicate with you about projects, services and relevant updates (with your consent where required); to maintain the security of our Services; and to comply with applicable laws. Some of this processing involves sharing information with trusted third-party service providers who support our operations — such as web hosting, analytics, email and communications providers — and with our affiliates, in each case subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations. We may also disclose information where required or permitted by law, or to protect the rights, property or safety of EMINO WORLD, our clients and others. We do not sell your personal information.

04 — Information protection

We take our responsibility to protect your information seriously and use reasonable and appropriate physical, technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee that our safeguards will be effective or sufficient.

05 — Accessing and correcting your information

We strive to keep the personal information we hold accurate, but we rely on you to ensure the information you provide is complete, accurate and up to date, and to inform us of any changes. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us using the details below.

06 — Your choices

You have choices about the information we collect and how we communicate with you. You may opt out of receiving marketing communications at any time by following the unsubscribe instructions in any message, or by contacting us. You can also adjust your device or browser settings to block or delete cookies, and opt out of certain analytics and advertising technologies. If you choose not to provide information that is necessary to deliver certain Services, you may not be able to use those Services.

07 — Other important information

Children. Our Services are not directed to children under 18 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Third-party websites. The Website may reference or link to third-party websites and services that we do not control. This Privacy Policy does not apply to them; please refer to the privacy notices of those third parties.

Business transfer. If we sell or transfer some or all of our business or assets, personal information we hold may be among the assets transferred.

08 — Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may modify, alter or update this Privacy Policy at any time, and any such change is effective upon our posting of the revised policy on the Website. We will use reasonable efforts to notify you of material changes. Your continued use of the Services following any change constitutes your acknowledgement of the amended Privacy Policy.
